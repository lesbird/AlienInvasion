# Alien Invasion — Operation Deep Descent: Game Guide

An alien force has overrun a sprawling underground military bunker. You start at the surface
access level and fight your way down through **30 sublevels**, collecting keycards, finding the
exit elevator on each floor, and finally destroying the **portal** the aliens are pouring out of
at the very bottom.

Everything in the game — every level layout, texture, 3D model, sound effect and music track — is
generated procedurally when the game runs. No two playthroughs are the same.

The game autosaves at the start of every sublevel; **Continue** on the title screen resumes there.
If you die, you restart the current sublevel with the health, armor, weapons and ammo you
entered it with.

## How a sublevel works

- You arrive in a **start room** and must reach the **exit elevator** — an alcove lit in green.
  Stand in it and press **E** to descend.
- The way to the elevator is blocked by **locked security doors** (1 on the first floor, up to 4
  deep down), colour-coded **red, blue, yellow and green**. Each needs the matching **keycard**,
  which always lies in the part of the level you can already reach.
- Plain doors open automatically as you approach.
- On mini-boss sublevels (5, 10, 15, 20, 25) the elevator stays **locked until the boss is dead**.
- The **automap** (Tab) fills in as you explore. Locked doors show in their key colour, opened
  secret areas in gold.

## The descent: areas and themes

| Sublevels | Look | Lighting |
|---|---|---|
| 1 – 7 | Clean military concrete, grey panels, yellow hazard stripes | Cool white fluorescents |
| 8 – 15 | Rust-stained industrial levels | Warm sodium light; rotating alarm beacons appear |
| 16 – 23 | Research labs and containment | White and red light; more broken lights |
| 24 – 30 | Alien-infested hive | Purple and green, glowing veins over every surface |

Each sublevel has a name: Ground Level – Surface Access, B1 – Security Checkpoint, B2 – Barracks,
B3 – Mess Hall, B4 – Armory, B5 – Motor Pool, B6 – Supply Depot, B7 – Communications,
B8 – Power Distribution, B9 – Water Reclamation, B10 – Maintenance Tunnels, B11 – Command Center,
B12 – Medical Wing, B13 – Detention Block, B14 – Deep Storage, B15 – Geothermal Plant,
B16 – Research Wing, B17 – Xenobiology Lab, B18 – Specimen Vault, B19 – Containment,
B20 – Quarantine Zone, B21 – Reactor Core, B22 – Excavation Site, B23 – The Old Mines,
B24 – Infested Sector, B25 – The Nest, B26 – The Hive, B27 – Brood Chambers,
B28 – Xeno Cathedral, and B29 – The Breach (the portal).

Levels get **bigger** and **more crowded** the deeper you go (about 6 aliens on the first floor,
up to 48 near the bottom). Aliens also get tougher with depth: by the last floors they have up to
**+60% health**, deal **+45% damage**, move 25% faster, react much faster, see and hear further,
aim better and lead their shots.

Rooms are furnished according to their purpose: offices with desks and monitors, control rooms
with consoles and blinking console banks, server rooms full of racks, barracks with bunks and
lockers, storage areas with shelves and barrels, labs with specimen tanks, and hive chambers full
of glowing alien egg pods. Furniture is solid cover; low furniture (desks, tables) can be shot over.

## Weapons

Weapons are picked up along the way and kept for the rest of the run. Four are guaranteed:
the **shotgun on sublevel 2**, the **assault rifle on 4**, the **grenade launcher on 7** and the
**rocket launcher on 11**. Others turn up as random pickups, in secret stashes, as mini-boss loot,
or are **dropped by aliens** (the alien weapons).

| # | Weapon | Ammo (per shot) | Damage | Fire rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Combat Knife** | — | 32 | 2.2 /s | Silent. **2.5× damage** against aliens that haven't noticed you. |
| 2 | **M9 Pistol** | Bullets (1) | 16 | 3.6 /s | Accurate starting sidearm. |
| 3 | **Combat Shotgun** | Shells (1) | 8 × 11 | 1.1 /s | Devastating up close. |
| 4 | **Assault Rifle** | Bullets (1) | 13 | 10 /s | Full-auto workhorse. |
| 5 | **Grenade Launcher** | Grenades (1) | 100, 4.5 m blast | 1.4 /s | Lobbed grenades bounce, then explode. |
| 6 | **Rocket Launcher** | Rockets (1) | 140, 4.5 m blast | 1.15 /s | Explodes on impact. Mind the blast radius. |
| 7 | **Xeno Plasma Rifle** *(alien)* | Cells (1) | 24 | 9 /s | Fast glowing bolts. Dropped by Xeno Troopers. |
| 8 | **Bio-Acid Launcher** *(alien)* | Cells (2) | 45, 2.6 m splash | 2.6 /s | Arcing acid globs. Dropped by Spitters. |
| 9 | **Arc Caster** *(alien)* | Cells (1) | 12 | 12 /s | Lightning that locks onto a target within 24 m and **chains to 3 more** (−25% per jump). Dropped by Wraiths. |
| 0 | **Void Cannon** *(alien)* | Cells (15) | 400, 7 m blast | 0.6 /s | A slow black singularity that obliterates groups. Dropped by Brutes and Overseers. |

**Ammo capacity:** 300 bullets, 60 shells, 40 grenades, 30 rockets, 400 cells
(×1.5 with the Ammo Backpack).

Your own explosions hurt you too, at reduced strength (40%). When a weapon runs dry the game
switches to your best weapon that still has ammo. Firing makes noise that alerts nearby aliens
(the knife is silent).

## Weapon upgrades

Every weapon has one **upgrade kit**, found in secret stashes (from sublevel 3) or as mini-boss
loot. A kit adapts to you: it becomes an upgrade for a weapon you **own and haven't upgraded yet**.
Upgraded weapons show in **gold with a "+"** on the HUD.

| Weapon | Upgrade | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Combat Knife | **Vibro-Blade** | Double damage, 30% faster |
| M9 Pistol | **Magnum Rounds** | 30 damage per shot (from 16), 15% faster |
| Combat Shotgun | **Dragon's Breath Shells** | 10 pellets × 14 damage (from 8 × 11) |
| Assault Rifle | **Rapid Fire Mod** | 15 shots/s (from 10), much tighter spread |
| Grenade Launcher | **Cluster Grenades** | Each grenade scatters 5 bomblets (38 damage each) after exploding |
| Rocket Launcher | **Twin Rockets** | Fires two rockets per shot for the cost of one |
| Xeno Plasma Rifle | **Overcharged Cells** | 36 damage per bolt (from 24), faster bolts |
| Bio-Acid Launcher | **Corrosive Mix** | +30% damage, +60% splash radius |
| Arc Caster | **Chain Storm** | Chains to 6 targets, 32 m range |
| Void Cannon | **Stabilised Core** | 10 cells per shot (from 15), 50% faster |

## Equipment: goggles and the flashlight

- **Flashlight (F):** a shoulder lamp lighting a cone ahead of you. It casts real shadows of
  aliens and furniture. Always available.
- **Night-Vision Goggles (N):** the screen turns into a bright green amplified image with film grain.
  **Every alien glows bright green**, including cloaked Stalkers. Guaranteed in the first secret
  stash of **sublevel 2**.
- **X-Ray Goggles (X):** the world turns cold blue, and within about 12 cells you can see **aliens
  through walls** (orange silhouettes) and **items and keycards through walls** (gold glints).
  That includes items hidden in secret rooms and Burrowers underground. Guaranteed in the first
  secret stash of **sublevel 5**.
- **Charge:** one pair of goggles at a time. Each runs on its own battery: a full charge is
  **15 minutes of use**, and it only drains while switched on. Batteries (from sublevel 3, one or
  two per level plus most secret stashes) add 7.5 minutes. The bottom-left meters show each
  charge and blink red when low. Goggles and their charge carry over between levels.

## Enemies

Aliens **patrol** until they see or hear you, react after a short delay (shorter deeper down),
then **hunt** you through the level, routing around doors and furniture. Ranged attackers strafe
and keep their distance. Most can be staggered by heavy hits. Health below is the base value;
it rises with depth.

| Alien | First seen | Health | Attack | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Skitter** | 1 | 30 | Bite (6) | Small, fast, many legs. Very common early, fades out by sublevel 20. |
| **Xeno Grunt** | 1 | 70 | Claw swipe (12) | Hulking two-legged brawler with clawed hands. |
| **Ceiling Crawler** | 4 | 55 | Bite (11) | Long-legged spider that **clings to the ceiling** and drops on you when you walk underneath or start shooting nearby. |
| **Spitter** | 3 | 60 | Acid glob (10, splash) | Bloated acid sac; spits arcing acid and keeps its distance. |
| **Xeno Trooper** | 5 | 100 | Plasma burst (3 × 7) | Armoured soldier with a plasma rifle. Fires 3-shot bursts. |
| **Bloater** | 6 | 45 | **Bursts** (50, 3.8 m acid blast) | Swollen with glowing acid. Charges you, swells for 0.7 s, then **explodes**. Also bursts when shot, hurting nearby aliens too, so kill it at range. |
| **Burrower** | 8 | 110 | Bite (18) | Centipede that **tunnels under the floor**. You see only a moving mound of earth and can't shoot it. It erupts right next to you, then digs back down when it loses track of you. Visible to X-ray goggles. |
| **Stalker** | 9 | 90 | Slash (16) | Fast four-legged hunter with **active camouflage**, almost invisible until close or attacking. Night vision reveals it. |
| **Wraith** | 12 | 120 | Psychic orb (14) | Floating jellyfish-like horror that hovers and fires slow orbs. |
| **Brute** | 16 | 500 | Smash (35), fire orb (30, splash) | Massive armoured beast with a heavy melee hit and explosive orbs. |
| **Overseer** | 23 | 900 | Triple orb spread (3 × 16) | Giant floating brain-eye with dangling tentacles. |

Aliens also **crawl out of wall vents** (see [Hazards](#hazards)) and pour from the portal on the
final level.

## Mini-bosses

Every fifth sublevel a mini-boss guards the room with the exit elevator. The **elevator stays
locked** until it dies. A health bar appears at the top of the screen once it spots you, and the
kill triggers a dramatic slow-motion moment.

| Boss | Sublevels | Health (base) | Abilities |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hive Queen** | 5, 20 | 2000 | Six-legged matriarch with an enormous glowing egg sac. Fires **5-way acid spreads**, slashes with scythe arms, and **breeds Skitters** from her sac every ~10 s (plus a Bloater on sublevel 20). |
| **Warlord** | 10, 25 | 3000 | Armoured giant with an arm cannon. Fires **3-orb explosive volleys**, **charges** across the room (a hit knocks you flying; if he smashes into a wall he's **dazed**), and **ground-slams** a damaging shockwave up close. |
| **The Mind** | 15 | 2600 | Floating brain with orbiting crystals. **Teleports** around you every few seconds, fires triple orbs, and releases **rings of 14 orbs** in every direction. |

**Boss loot (guaranteed):** a weapon you don't have yet (or, if you have them all, an upgrade
kit or ammo backpack), a Xeno-Serum, a Kevlar Vest and two boxes of ammo for your best weapon.

## The final battle

**Sublevel 30, The Breach**, is a huge arena around the **Alien Portal**, a swirling vortex
inside a ring of spikes. The portal is protected by a **shield** powered by **four Shield
Pylons**, crystal towers linked to it by beams of energy.

1. Destroy all four pylons (600 health each) to drop the shield.
2. Destroy the portal itself (3000 health).

Meanwhile the portal keeps **summoning aliens**: one every 10 s while shielded, every 6.5 s
once the shield is down, up to 10 at a time. The arena is stocked with cells, rockets, a medkit
and a vest. When the portal collapses, every remaining alien dies with it and the mission is won.

## Secrets

Every sublevel hides **1 to 4 secret stashes** behind fake wall panels. A secret panel uses the
normal wall texture but is a **slightly different shade**. Face it and press **E** to slide it open.

A stash always holds:
- a **goodie**: a Weapon Upgrade Kit, Night-Vision or X-Ray Goggles, a Damage Amplifier, an Ammo
  Backpack, or an early weapon
- **health** (a Medkit or Xeno-Serum)
- **armor** (Armor Plating or a Kevlar Vest)
- **two ammo pickups**
- often a **battery**

The HUD shows **SECRETS found / total**, and the level summary counts them toward your score.
Secret rooms stay hidden on the automap until opened. X-ray goggles show stash items glowing
through the wall.

## Hazards

| Hazard | Where | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Red explosive barrels** | Groups of 1–3, mostly in storage areas | 30 health: two pistol shots or one shotgun blast. Damaged barrels leak flame, then explode after a split-second flash: **100 damage in a 5 m radius** to you *and* aliens, and they set off nearby barrels in a chain. Any explosion or alien projectile can set them off. |
| **Wall vents** | Many rooms | Aliens crawl out when you're nearby. Glowing eyes sometimes stare out from behind the slats. **Shoot or blast a vent** (60 health) to seal it for good. |
| **Steam vents** | Floor grates from sublevel 2 | Blast a scalding column of steam every 5.5 s for 1.6 s. Don't stand on them when they hiss. |
| **Live cables** | Hanging from walls from sublevel 4 | Spark and flicker blue; touching one gives a 14-damage shock and throws you back. |
| **Acid pools** | Glowing green puddles from sublevel 16 | Burn you while you stand in them. |

## Level events

- **Lockdown ambush (from sublevel 3):** one room per level (two from sublevel 15) is a trap. Step
  inside and **red energy barriers seal every entrance**, a klaxon wails, and a wave of aliens
  (5 + sublevel ÷ 3 of them) **crawls out of the room's vents**. Wrecking the vents makes the rest
  teleport in instead. Kill them all to lift the barriers and collect a **medkit and ammo**. If the
  last few won't come out, they retreat after 20 s and the lockdown ends anyway.
- **Power failure (about 40% of sublevels 6–27):** partway through the level the generators die.
  Lights flicker out, only pulsing **red emergency lamps** remain, and it stays dark for 35–55 s
  until the power stutters back. Night vision and the flashlight are your friends.
- **Self-destruct (sublevels 28 and 29):** picking up the **last keycard** starts the base's
  self-destruct sequence. A countdown (80–220 s, depending on how far the elevator is) appears with
  klaxons, rumbling and shaking. Reach the elevator before it hits zero or the base blows up with
  you in it.
- **Alarm beacons (from sublevel 8):** rotating red, amber or purple lights sweep rooms with
  twin beams, throwing moving shadows of the aliens.

## Score and ranking

Every sublevel ends with a score breakdown and a letter **rank**:

| Category | Points |
|---|---|
| Kills | 100 per alien |
| Boss | 2500 for killing the mini-boss |
| Secrets | 750 per secret found |
| Time | 20 points per second under 1.5 × par |
| Accuracy | up to 2000 (shots that hit ÷ shots fired) |
| Deaths | −1000 per restart of the sublevel |

**Par time** is 75 s + 10 s per sublevel number (+60 s on boss levels).

**Rank** weighs kill percentage (35%), secrets found (25%), speed against par (25%) and accuracy
(15%), minus a penalty for each death: **S**, A, B, C or D.

Your **run total** accumulates across the campaign and is shown on the victory screen. Your
**best score and rank for each sublevel** are saved (`scores.json`, next to the save file), and
the summary flags a **NEW BEST!**

## Presentation

- **Lighting:** up to 32 dynamic lights at once, with flickering and broken lights, glowing
  emissive screens and LEDs, and fog that thickens with depth.
- **Shadows:** real-time shadows of aliens from nearby lights and rotating beacons, plus
  flashlight shadows of aliens and furniture.
- **Surfaces:** bump-mapped walls, floors and ceilings, with an alien infestation overlay that
  spreads as you go deeper.
- **Bloom:** HDR glow around lights, screens, muzzle flashes and explosions (toggle with **B**).
- **Gore and feedback:**
  - hit markers, a red kill marker, and a brief slow-motion on every kill
  - bullet holes, scorch marks and alien-blood splatter that stay on walls and floors
  - limbs that come off, and big or explosive kills that blow aliens into chunks that bounce and settle
- **Audio:**
  - every sound is synthesised: weapons, alien growls and screeches, doors, the elevator, klaxons, steam
  - positional sound
  - a procedurally generated music track for every sublevel
- **HUD:** health, armor, ammo, weapon slots, keycards, kills, time, secrets, goggle charge,
  damage-amp timer, boss health, lockdown and self-destruct banners, plus the automap.

## Tips

- **Search the walls.** Secrets hold the best gear in the game: upgrades, goggles, backpacks.
  Look for wall panels with a slightly different shade, and use X-ray goggles to spot stash items.
- **Use the barrels.** Lure groups of aliens next to red barrels and set them off from a distance.
  Keep your own distance.
- **Kill Bloaters early** and at range. Never let one reach you, and don't shoot one next to you.
- **Look up** when entering rooms from sublevel 4 on, and watch for moving mounds of earth from 8.
- **Shoot vents** as soon as you see them in rooms you'll be fighting in, especially before a
  lockdown springs.
- **Save the knife for sneak attacks.** It deals 2.5× damage to unaware aliens.
- **Dodge the Warlord's charge** sideways and let him hit the wall, then unload while he's dazed.
- **Save goggle charge**: switch night vision on for power failures and dark hive levels, and use
  x-ray in short bursts to scout.
- **Be quick** on sublevels 28 and 29: clear the level *before* grabbing the last keycard.
